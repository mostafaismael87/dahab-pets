Fleas and ticks: where to start

Simple signs help you catch the problem early, and your vet can guide the rest.

10 October 2026

Signs to watch for

More scratching than usual, small black specks in the coat, or ticks around the ears and between the toes.

Check after walks

Comb the coat with a flea comb and look closely after walks in gardens and open areas.

Choose treatment with a vet

The dose depends on species, weight and age. Dog products can be dangerous to cats, so use only a product made for your pet.

Clean the home too

Wash your pet's bedding and clean the places where it sleeps so the problem does not return.
